Version: 1.2.0
Date: 2026-10-15
  Changes:
    - Reworked parameter generation: prototypes are cloned from the vanilla parameter-1 prototypes with an up-front existence check, so a missing vanilla prototype now fails with a readable error instead of a cryptic crash.
    - All new prototypes are now registered in a single batched data:extend call.
    - Letter parameters (A-Z) now have an explicit prefixed sort order so they sort deterministically after the vanilla 0-9 and still-not-enough-parameters' 10-99, instead of splitting the alphabet in half when both mods are enabled.
